# 3h12 — notes for Claude

## How the owner uses it
- The owner reads **every app as a Safari web app on iPhone** (added to the home screen). Test and design for
  **iOS Safari / WebKit** first: Chrome rendering is not proof. Avoid SVG features WebKit handles badly
  (masks over complex or `<use>` content, filters inside masked groups); prefer plain gradients and opacity overlays.
- No zoom, no sideways sliding: the page must stay exactly screen-wide.

## Standing rules (from the owner)
- English only. Stories are the authors' original text, verbatim; the owner supplies every story.
- DEV code: BURGER.
- No text "windows"; no dissolves for people appearing or leaving; no text shaking.
- Sound: no looping drips or "white-noise waves"; music must not loop annoyingly; Lynn and creatures only when the text mentions them.
- Each story should feel like its own project (fonts, layout, look), not the same template every night.
